Core Values

The five principles that guide CMSR-TZ in every program and partnership

What Guides Us

Our Core Values

These five values shape every decision, partnership, and action at CMSR-TZ.

Service

We put the community first and the organization before ourselves. We are dedicated to the Vision and Mission, and we delight ourselves in our commitment to the needs of our target population.

Teamwork

Listening to and respecting each other whilst working together to achieve mutual beneficial results. Everyone has strengths which we value and will use whenever possible. All technical team meetings include a progress report from everyone and requests for help when needed.

Collaboration

Collaborating in and outside the country with various CSOs, NGOs, Government, private sector, faith-based organizations, and the entire population of Tanzania to deliver best practices in community development initiatives.

Integrity

We uphold the highest standards of conduct. We seek and speak the truth to our partners and to our target population. We honor those who have come before us and those with whom we collaborate today.

Excellence

Always doing what we plan, striving for excellence and quality in everything we do. Our motto: "Quality before Quantity delights the target population even staying within the budget limitation."

"We believe that change is possible."

– CMSR-TZ Mission Statement
