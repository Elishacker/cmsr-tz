Water & Sanitation

Water & Sanitation Sector

Bringing Clean Water to Rural Communities

CMSR-TZ builds water infrastructure and promotes sanitation practices that give rural communities and schools lasting access to clean water in Dodoma and Kagera Regions.

Rulenge Water Infrastructure

As part of the Rulenge Project in Kagera Region, CMSR-TZ drilled a deep water well and installed a solar-powered pump system, bringing clean water access to a community that previously relied on unsafe sources.

  • Deep water well drilling completed
  • Solar-powered pump system installed
  • Water distribution stations (DP) constructed
  • Pump house & machinery depot built
Support Water Projects

Water Access at Partner Schools

Water storage tanks support hygiene and sanitation facilities at secondary schools involved in the PMHM menstrual health program, helping students access clean water for handwashing and sanitary needs.

  • Water storage tanks installed at partner schools
  • Linked with menstrual hygiene & sanitation facilities
  • Supports PMHM schools across Dodoma Region
