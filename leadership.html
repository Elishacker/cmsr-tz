Leadership

The people driving CMSR-TZ's mission in Tanzania

Josephine K. Pamilla

Board Chairperson

Statement from the Board Chairperson

"The CMSR-TZ team performed very well in the financial year despite significant challenges of financial support and other social commitments in the community development projects."

Before the formal presentation of the report, I would, on behalf of the Board Members, like to offer a sincere thank you to every employee of CMSR-Tanzania for their dedication and hard work in the past year 2025. They have, with their commitment and dedicated efforts, steered the organization through another year of challenges including the lack of permanent donors for fund support in implementation of social projects.

As stated, I believe that we have an opportunity to do better than what we did and ensure that our targets in the process of implementing community-based projects are on board for sustainable development, particularly for the rural population.

Despite continuing economic headwinds, CMSR-Tanzania is well-positioned for further growth and I look forward with confidence to the year ahead.

Francis G. Manghundi

Executive Director

Statement from the Executive Director

"The year 2025 was of success because we managed to implement various project activities as intended."

The strategy of CMSR-TZ aims at community development efforts to eliminate various problems including poverty. Throughout 2025, we have continued to invest in implementing community-based projects that address health, education, water and sanitation, and agriculture sectors.

CMSR-TZ has continued to collaborate closely with the Ministry of Health, TAMISEMI, District Councils, and various development partners including CEI, AICS, CUAMM, COPE, AVSI, World Friends, and the private sector.

My gratitude goes to all Board Members for their valuable support in board meetings and individual advice to management. I am very confident that with their continuing commitment, determination, positive attitude and teamwork, the CMSR-TZ team will achieve its Vision, Mission, and Core Values.
