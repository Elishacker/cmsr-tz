Board of Directors

The governing body overseeing CMSR-TZ's mission and programs

Governance

Board of Directors

The CMSR-TZ Board has 6 members and serves as the organization's governing body. Board members hold office for three (3) years and may be re-appointed based on active participation and performance in Board duties.

Board Structure & Responsibilities

Composition

The Board is composed of a Chairperson, Vice Chairperson, and Board Members, forming a total of 6 members. Members were selected based on recommendations from relevant Consultants.

Term of Office

Board members hold office for three (3) years and may be re-appointed based on active participation and performance in Board duties for three years.

Core Function

The Board serves as the organization's governing body, ensuring CMSR-TZ efficiently achieves its goals and operates in accordance with its Constitution and Tanzanian law.

2025 Performance

The Board is currently in its second year of active service. It has been a reliable support in the development of CMSR-TZ, providing strategic oversight across all programs.
