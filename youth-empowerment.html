Youth Empowerment

Youth Empowerment Sector

Investing in Tanzania's Next Generation

Through the Rulenge Project in Kagera Region, CMSR-TZ equips young people with legal knowledge, rights awareness, and life skills to build safer, more independent futures.

Juvenile Justice Studies

As part of the Rulenge Project – "Help for Women Entrepreneurship with an Ecological Address in Rulenge Area, Kagera Region, Ngara District" – CMSR-TZ delivers juvenile justice studies that help young people understand their legal rights and responsibilities.

  • Location: Rulenge, Ngara District, Kagera Region
  • Focus: Legal rights, responsibilities & child protection
Support Youth Programs

Gender & Minor Rights Training

Community training sessions on gender equality and minor rights build awareness and protection for young people, complementing the entrepreneurship and vocational skills training available to youth in the community.

  • Gender and minor rights training sessions
  • Linked with entrepreneurship & commercial training
  • Delivered alongside community health education
