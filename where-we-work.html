Where We Work

CMSR-TZ operates across Tanzania Mainland and Zanzibar, with a special focus on rural communities in three key regions.

Dodoma Region

Our headquarters & primary area of operations

Dodoma City is the headquarters of CMSR-TZ and the region where the majority of our programs are implemented. We work closely with Dodoma City Council, district authorities, schools, and health facilities.

Shule Program

8 secondary schools: Wella, Viwandani, Lukundo, Itega, Chikole, Miyuji, Makole and more. 245+ students sponsored.

Health – PMHM

14 secondary schools: Bereko, Kisese, Kikore, Hurui, Masange, Bumbuta, Abeid Karume, Kiteo, Kinyasi, Busi, Loo, Intela, Kalamba, R.M Lowassa.

Health – IMaNHC

Dodoma City Council health facilities. Strengthening CEmONC (emergency obstetric & neonatal care) services.

SWALA – Women Empowerment

Chikopelo Bwawani village, Bahi District. NGUVU KAZI group of 7 women producing kitenge products.

Kagera Region

Ngara District – Rulenge Area

In Ngara District, Kagera Region, CMSR-TZ leads the comprehensive Rulenge project, working in partnership with the Archdiocese of Mbeya and Good Fortune Company Ltd.

Agriculture Training

Jatropha cultivation training, agricultural field practice, and entrepreneurship for women in rural Kagera.

Water Infrastructure

Deep water well drilled, solar pump installed, pump house constructed, and water distribution stations built for the community.

Solar Energy

Solar energy system installed in classrooms and for the water pump system to power the Rulenge project facilities.

Infrastructure

Classroom & office building, FUSO Truck, soap production machines, oil machines, warehouse (SILOS), and security house.

Zanzibar

IMaNHC multi-country project

CMSR-TZ served as the lead organization for the IMaNHC (Improving Maternal and Neonatal Health Care) multi-country project. The Zanzibar component was implemented by our partner CUAMM under CMSR-Tanzania's lead.

IMaNHC – Zanzibar Component

Partner: CUAMM. Focus: Improving maternal and neonatal health care in Zanzibar facilities. Status: Successfully completed in 2025.
