Agriculture & Livelihoods

Agriculture Programs

CMSR-TZ leads two agriculture and livelihoods projects providing training, infrastructure, and empowerment to rural communities in Dodoma and Kagera Regions.

Sekondari ya Kilimo Project

The Sekondari ya Kilimo Project is an agricultural secondary school construction and development initiative in Tanzania. The project includes:

  • Construction of classroom and office buildings
  • Installation of solar energy systems
  • Student hostel refurbishment
  • Water pump and solar panel system setup
  • Furniture procurement (classrooms and offices)
  • Security house construction

Construction is progressing well, with significant milestones achieved in 2025.

Rulenge Project – Kagera Region

"Help for Women Entrepreneurship with an Ecological Address in Rulenge Area, Kagera Region, Ngara District, Tanzania"

A comprehensive project covering multiple areas of development:

  • Agricultural training (Jatropha cultivation & field practice)
  • Community health education
  • Entrepreneurship & commercial training
  • Juvenile justice studies
  • Deep water well drilling & solar pump system
  • Water distribution stations constructed
  • Pump house & machinery depot constructed
  • Soap production & cold press oil machines purchased
  • Basketry making & Yagayaga Petroleum jelly production
  • FUSO Truck purchased for logistics
  • Solar energy system installed in classrooms
  • Main gate & fence constructed
  • Warehouse (SILOS) construction
  • Gender and minor rights training

Water Infrastructure

Deep water well drilled in Rulenge. Solar-powered pump and water distribution stations constructed, bringing clean water access to the community.

Solar Energy

Solar energy systems installed in classrooms and for water pumping in both the Sekondari ya Kilimo project and the Rulenge project in Kagera Region.

Skills & Enterprise

Women receive entrepreneurship training and production equipment (soap machines, oil machines) to build sustainable income-generating activities.
