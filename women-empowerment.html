Women Empowerment – SWALA Program

Water and Sanitation

Water and Sanitation

Since 2016, the SWALA Program has been building women's economic capacity through tailoring activities at Chikopelo Bwawani village, Bahi District, Dodoma Region.

The NGUVU KAZI group – a group of 7 women – is always engaged in sewing linen products and various materials under the close supervision of CMSR-Tanzania, supported by CMSR Italy (Centro Mondialità Sviluppo Reciproco).

  • Shopping bags and handbags
  • Favours bags and pouches
  • Pencil cases and small accessories
  • Washable sanitary pads for PMHM program

Products are made using kitenge fabrics and delivered to Bottega del Mondo in Livorno, Italy.

Main Objective

Building women's economic capacity through tailoring activities and empowering women to be self-reliant and self-employed in Bahi District.

Swala Collection

The Swala collection includes handmade items produced using kitenge fabrics: shopping bags, favour bags, pouches, pencil cases, and accessories. Production takes place in Chikopelo Bwawani.

International Market

Products are exported to CMSR-Italy and sold at Bottega del Mondo in Livorno, Italy, connecting Tanzanian craftswomen with an international market.

Menstrual Hygiene

The group also produces washable sanitary pads for secondary school students as part of the PMHM (Menstrual Health Management) program, promoting hygiene and school retention for girls.

Social Media Promotion

The program is promoted through social media (Instagram, Facebook) and storytelling videos created by Italian volunteers to expand visibility of the SWALA collection.

Community Impact

840 favour bags produced per production cycle. The program plans to continue expanding to include more women and promote the SWALA brand internationally.
